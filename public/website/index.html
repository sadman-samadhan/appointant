AM Barbershop

At AM Barbershop we know you want to look and feel your best.

That takes regular grooming from barbers who know what they are doing.

Our shop on York Street is all black and gold, with comfortable chairs and a relaxed, friendly feel. Come in for a quick clean-up or take your time with the full treatment.

We offer fades, textured cuts and wolf cuts, kids cuts, head shaves, hot towel shaves, beard trims and facial massage. Our signature AM Treatment brings it all together: a haircut, shave or beard trim, face massage, scalp massage and shampoo.

Here’s how it works: book online or walk in, tell your barber what you’re after, and walk out the door looking fresh and feeling good.
